Privacy policy

Effective 29 September 2026

In short: Sorced has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone. If, and only if, you agree, the game sends anonymous play stats and crash reports that help me improve it. You can turn that off at any time.

Who makes Sorced

Sorced is made by Ruben Depover, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here. You can reach me at ruben.depover@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your save game (progress, stars, unlocks, settings) is stored only on your iPhone. It isn't uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app deletes it.

What is sent, and only with your permission

After your first duel, Sorced asks whether you want to share play stats and crash reports. Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing is sent if you choose No thanks. If you choose Share, the game sends:

  • Gameplay events: which screens you open, which duels you start and how they end (score, stars, duration, the runes and spells you drew, how often a stroke wasn't recognised), seals and duels you complete, Gauntlet runs, upgrades, and changes to settings.
  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed.
  • Device details: device model, iOS version, app version and language.
  • A random ID created for this installation, so events from the same game can be counted together. It isn't linked to your name, your Apple ID or any other app.

What is never collected

No name, email address or account; no contacts, photos or precise location; no advertising identifier; and no tracking across other apps or websites. Your IP address is discarded when the data arrives, and no location is derived from it.

Who processes it

The stats are stored with PostHog, an analytics service, in its EU data centre, on my behalf and only for the purposes below. The data isn't sold or shared with anyone else.

Why

To find duels that are too hard or too easy, strokes the game misreads, and crashes, so they can be fixed. The legal basis is your consent.

How long it's kept

Stats are kept for 12 months, then deleted.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing: turn off Share play stats in Sorced's Settings. Nothing more is sent.
  • Delete what was sent: write to the address above with the Stats ID shown in Settings, and I'll delete the data linked to it.
  • You can also ask what data is held about that ID, or complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Sorced isn't aimed at children under 13, and the optional stats contain nothing that identifies a person.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
