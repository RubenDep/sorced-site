Write to CONTACT ADDRESS TO COME. It helps to include your iPhone model, the app version (at the bottom of Settings), and which duel you were in.

Common questions

My rune isn't recognised. Practise it on the Grimoire's Practice pad, which shows the rune it read and how cleanly you drew it. Settings → Reading → Generous reading accepts less precise strokes (and grades them a little more kindly).

The game runs slowly or gets warm. Settings → Graphics lets you pick a lower quality, or turn off bloom and post effects.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone? Your save is part of your iPhone backup, so restoring a backup to the new phone brings it along.

How do I stop sharing play stats? Settings → Data → Share play stats. See the privacy policy.
